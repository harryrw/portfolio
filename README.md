## ✨ Features & Fun Details

### 🦜 The Chameleon Toucan
In the top-left of the navbar sits a custom Toucan home button. Toggle between light and dark mode to watch its habitat transform from a sun-drenched tropical jungle into a starry midnight canopy.

### 🌅 Time-Traveling Dynamic Avatar
On the home page, the illustrated portrait of Harry sits in front of a living, breathing landscape that automatically syncs with your local time of day:
- **Dawn (Sunrise)**: Soft morning amber and indigo horizon
- **Day (Midday)**: Bright blue skies, radiant sun, and rolling green hills
- **Dusk (Sunset)**: Fiery orange, rose, and twilight purple ridges
- **Night**: Deep slate skies under a quiet, glowing moon

### ☀️🌙 Live Celestial Distance Tracker
Why settle for a boring "Online" status when your browser can track the cosmos?
- **Light Mode**: Measures your live orbital distance from **Earth to the Sun** in real time (approx. 148+ million km), calculated using Keplerian orbital mechanics.
- **Dark Mode**: Automatically flips to track the live distance from **Earth to the Moon** (approx. 373,000+ km), accounting for real-time lunar orbital perturbations. Watch the kilometer counter tick live every second!

### 🏀 Featured Production: The Key
A concept and template for an interactive basketball court discovery and pickup matchmaking platform:
- Named after **The Key** — the painted free-throw area under the hoop where games are won and lost.
- Features a top-down street map view with custom basketball court pins, crowd-verified court conditions (rim heights, net types, night lighting), and real-time pickup lobbies for spontaneous 3v3 and 5v5 runs.

### 🚀 Featured Production: GamingLANDR.com
A cosmonaut-themed intelligence radar that pulls gaming industry rumors and leaks across Bluesky, Reddit, and X into a unified stream:
- Showcases a website mockup tracking Valve's top-secret **Project HLX (Half-Life 3)**.
- Features the classic encircled orange $\lambda$ icon with the superscript **3**, the iconic G-Man quote *“Rise and shine, Mr Freeman”*, and community veracity scoring.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Next.js 15 & React 19**: Lightning-fast App Router with instant server-rendered pages
- **Tailwind CSS v4**: Theme-aware color palettes, custom gradients, and zero-runtime styles
- **TypeScript**: Full type safety from site configuration to media schemas
- **Orbital Mathematics**: Lightweight astronomical formulas for live Earth-Sun and Earth-Moon distances
- **Vercel**: Edge deployments with zero-downtime CI/CD from GitHub
